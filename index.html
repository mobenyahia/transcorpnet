Culture guide

Transcorp's five core values, our recognition programme, corporate discounts, and the social heartbeat of the group.

Our five core values

Trust

We do what we say, say what we mean, and build confidence through consistency and transparency across every market.

Customer focus

We exist to serve our customers and put their needs at the centre of every decision we make as a group.

Excellence

We set high standards, pursue continuous improvement, and never settle for good enough in any of our operations.

Innovation

We challenge convention, embrace new ideas, and build better ways of working for our people and our customers.

Sustainability

We build for the long term — for our people, our customers, and the communities we operate in across the GCC.
